Decimancer

A free tool for converting geographic coordinates from various formats into decimal degrees. Upload a CSV or Excel file, and Decimancer will detect your coordinate columns and append standardised decimal degree values.

Supported input formats:

  • DMS with symbols: 33°51'54"S
  • DMS space-separated: 33 51 54 S
  • Decimal minutes: 33°51.9'S
  • Decimal degrees: -33.865 (pass-through)
  • Mixed formats within the same column
  • Encoding-mangled coordinates from legacy spreadsheets
